Eight indicators. One score.

These are the TradingView indicators the bots port and run on daily bars. Each one that fires adds points to the CALL or PUT side of a setup's score. Click any card for the free deep dive with a real chart.

GAP FILL
Gaps

Price gaps between one bar and the next. A new gap fires when today's low clears yesterday's high (or the mirror) by at least 0.30 × the 14-bar average range. A filled gap fires when price re-enters a gap that is still open, read as a reversal.

UT Bot

An ATR trailing stop that flips with the trend. Key value 1.0 and a 10-bar ATR. Fires buy when the close crosses above the trailing stop and sell when it crosses below.

EMA stack

Trend alignment across moving averages. Golden or death cross of the 50 and 200 EMAs; the 20 > 50 > 100 > 200 fan (or the reverse); price above or below the 200 EMA when the fan isn't firing.

AlgoAlpha Zero Lag

A lag-reduced trend line with volatility bands. Zero-lag EMA of length 70 with bands at 1.2 × the highest 70-bar ATR over 210 bars. Entries fire when price re-crosses the line inside a trend; a trend change fires when price breaks a band.

Trend Reversal Probability

How unusually long the current swing has run. An oscillator of 5- and 34-bar averages tracks swing phases; the length of the current phase is compared with past phases and turned into a probability. Fires above 0.84, and as extreme above 0.98.

How they combine

CALL and PUT points are added up separately, alongside classic signals like RSI, Bollinger touches and volume surges, and the larger side wins. Each signal's points are multiplied by a learned weight. A setup needs at least 35 points from at least three signals, or two signals when one of them is a premium signal, and it must still clear every entry gate before it posts.

The Bots → How a signal is scored

How a signal is scored

Phantom Bot adds up points for CALL and PUT separately. Each indicator or classic signal that fires adds its points to one side, multiplied by a learned weight. The larger side wins; ties are rejected.

From points to a signal

  • Agreement bonus: +10 when three or more signals agree.
  • Regime: the market regime scales each side (for example ×1.2 for CALLs and ×0.7 for PUTs when risk is on).
  • Volatility: ×0.85 when the VIX is above 25.
  • Minimum: at least 35 points from at least three signals, or two signals when one is a premium signal (UT Bot, QQE MOD, a gap-and-go, an SMC break, a 50/200 cross, a Zero Lag trend change, an extreme reversal probability or a MACD_X divergence).
  • Confidence: HIGH at 80 or more, MEDIUM at 50 or more. Setups under 80 must repeat in the same direction on the next scan.

Where the weights come from

A weekly replay of two years of daily bars grades the classic signals (RSI, Bollinger, MACD, volume, EMA trend): a signal "wins" if the stock moves 1% its way within five days. A separate learner grades every signal, including the eight ported indicators, on the bots' own closed paper trades over the last 60 days. Small weight changes apply automatically; large ones wait for human approval.

The Bots → Entry gates

Entry gates

A high score is not enough. Before any paper trade opens, the setup has to clear every gate. A gate whose data cannot be read blocks the entry.

  • Regular hours only, and not in the first 15 minutes
  • No entries within 30 minutes of scheduled macro events
  • No entries 5 days before or 1 day after earnings
  • VIX above 30 blocks entries; above 25 cuts size to a quarter, above 20 to half
  • Bid-ask spread under 4% (scalp 3%, weekly 7%, LEAP 10%) and premium of at least $0.50
  • An out-of-the-money ceiling of 2.5%, and no out-of-the-money contracts with 0–2 days left
  • A live quote is required
  • Trade with the trend: no PUTs in an uptrend, no CALLs in a downtrend
  • Position caps: 15 day trades, 8 scalps, 8 weeklies and 5 LEAPs open at once
  • Day-trade entries stop at 3:00 PM ET; weekly and LEAP entries at 3:30 PM ET

CHANGELOG.

What changed in the bots, the Discord and the site, newest first.

October 2026

Courses, store and Discord tiers

NewImproved
  • 20 courses with 13 lessons each, a graded capstone, charts and quizzes; lessons 1 and 2 of every course are free
  • Four bundles (Beginner, Intermediate, Markets, Expert All-Access) on one Stripe catalog
  • Plans are now Discord tiers: Rookie (free), Pro Trader and Elite, each listing the channels it unlocks
September 2026

Link Discord, safer exits

NewImprovedFix
  • Link your Discord account from the Account page; Pro Trader and Elite roles are assigned when you subscribe
  • Exits no longer wait on Discord or the market-data cache, so a slow post can't delay a close
  • If a gate's data can't be read the bot keeps the last good answer, and a gate that has never been read blocks entries
  • Donchian breakout entries turned off after they were the weakest family in the paper book
  • #flow-alerts looks back 45 minutes because flow data arrives 15–21 minutes late
August 2026

Stricter promotion gates

NewImproved
  • A strategy must beat random entry and simply holding the asset, on deployed Sharpe, before it can post signals
  • Paper fills are charged the bid-ask spread the trade would actually pay
  • End-of-day force-close keyed to days to expiry, not trade type
July 2026

New channels and portfolio-level risk caps

NewImproved
  • #futures and #macro-news channels; Bullseye weekly signals moved to their own #weekly channel
  • #macro-news event wire: weekly economic calendar plus the actual figure when each release drops
  • Regime director feeds the SPY 200-day trend into both bots' scores
  • Per-bot heat and net-delta caps limit how much risk the paper book can stack in one direction
  • Signal channels are read-only so alerts don't get buried
June 2026

Weekly swings and trend-side entries

NewImprovedFix
  • Swing/weekly strategy with a near-52-week-high variant
  • In-the-money oversold-bounce scalp, limited to the most liquid names
  • Trend detection: entries lean toward the side of the prevailing trend
  • Earnings checks fail closed: no entry when the earnings date can't be confirmed
  • Unified daily recap posted to #trade-recap
May 2026

Layered exits and the morning brief

NewImproved
  • ATR-based exits and volume confirmation, enabled after backtest validation
  • #morning-brief at 8:45 AM ET adds today's and tomorrow's macro events
  • #trade-log posts every paper open and close as an image card
